## Principles and evidence

- Patience, controlled approach and recognizing a clean chance to win the ball:
  [The FA: introducing defending](https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/introducing-defending).
- Covering depends on actual positions, angles and distances; support is not a
  guarantee of winning possession:
  [England Football: covering and recovering](https://learn.englandfootball.com/articles-and-resources/coaching/resources/2023/What-is-covering-and-recovering).
- Look before receiving and protect the ball when pressure arrives:
  [The FA: receiving under pressure](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/education-hub/files/pdf/receiving-under-pressure---session-plan.ashx?la=en).
- Watching both the cross and the assigned opponent:
  [FIFA: defending principles](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/media/native/test/FIFA_Session_Plan_Boothroyd2.pdf).
- Dangerous or reckless contact is not excused by touching the ball first;
  deliberate return passes are played to the keeper's feet:
  [IFAB Law 12](https://theifab.com/laws/latest/fouls-and-misconduct/).
- Shielding requires the body between opponent and ball, within playing distance;
  holding or pushing the opponent is not shielding:
  [FIFA: strength through shielding](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/futsal/fitness/strength-through-shielding.php)
  and IFAB Law 12 above.
- Build-out-line release triggers vary. Compare the official
  [AYSO referee guidance](https://www.ayso2.com/MY2024_Ref/Regional/Regional_Referee_Course_Lesson_Plan.pdf)
  with [NJYS's league guidance](https://www.mnjysa.org/news_article/show/1180103).
  NJYS could not be fetched during this recheck (HTTP 403); it is a historical
  comparison, not newly verified evidence. The fetched AYSO 2024 course states
  release from the hands, and its unrelated old six-second text is not used here.
  The new distribution pictures start opponents behind the line, then explicitly
  jump forward to after John's first touch, with pressure live in the given
  picture. They do not claim first touch is the universal release trigger.
- For age-appropriate combinations, England Football's current guidance says
  collaboration grows in importance for 9-11 year olds while individual ball
  skill remains in progress: [connecting creatively with others](https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/how-to-connect-and-combine-creatively-with-others).
- England Football's [7v7 guidance](https://learn.englandfootball.com/articles-and-resources/coaching/resources/2025/How-to-play-and-manage-7v7-games)
  and [9v9 guidance](https://learn.englandfootball.com/articles-and-resources/coaching/resources/2025/How-to-play-and-manage-9v9-games)
  confirm that formats have distinct restart and retreat-line rules. The game
  keeps the user's stated 7v7 line practice explicit and leaves other local rules
  to the coach. The 9v9 pictures focus on nearby decisions, not fixed formations.
- In England's 2026-27 guidance, U10-U11 are 7v7 and U12-U13 are 9v9; the user's
  actual 7v7 report is retained, and no age format is inferred for another
  association: [format overview](https://learn.englandfootball.com/articles-and-resources/coaching/resources/2022/what-is-a-format-in-football).

- Pressure should remove time and space when a carrier is in shooting range;
  covering and tracking depend on who actually confronts the ball:
  [FIFA: defensive coordination in small groups](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/talent-coach-programme/zonal-defending/developing-defensive-coordination-in-small-groups.php).
  This supports the decision principles; the game adapts them to short local
  pictures for John rather than teaching the full session's formations.

- Being near A only helps if John can influence the shot or its angle:
  [FIFA: assessing the defender's influence](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/futsal/coaching-tips/part-3-reading-the-game-before-intervening.php).
  This futsal goalkeeper seminar is used for its explicit observation about
  defender influence; its goalkeeper positioning/handling instructions are not
  transferred to John. The distance comparison is an authored adaptation.

These sources support the principles. They do not individually endorse the

## How to read the picture review

The preferred action fits the explicitly stated picture. Reasonable options
are accepted alternatives, not errors. Risky options require checking the
explanation; their illustrated routes are not predictions of certain failure.
Only nearby players are shown. Percentage coordinates and animation duration
are teaching aids, not physical measurements of children's running or kicking.

For sliding, picture 28.3 is a sliding interception of a rolling shot with the
opponent clear of the route; it does not teach sliding through another player.
For long kicks, John is an outfield player; these are not goalkeeper punts.
