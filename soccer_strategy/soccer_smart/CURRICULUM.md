Current implementation: the original 36 scenes and seven cards are retained,
and Book companion adds 29 scenes and 24 cards. The leaf-level
[coverage audit](BOOK_COVERAGE.md) is the current runtime inventory: 56 original
adaptations plus one deferred heading entry across 57 indexed topics.
No chapter body is verified. The grouped plan and first-release backlog below
record the earlier design and must not be read as proof of complete chapters.

This is a whole-book theme plan, not verified instruction from the whole book.
Group labels are paraphrased. Locators come from [S3](SOURCES.md); all groups
G01-G22 are INDEX_ONLY / FULL_TEXT_PENDING. Activities, priorities and game
connections are ORIGINAL. Group G23 uses only the publisher overview.

## Technique and adult cards

These are ORIGINAL activity concepts, not exercises copied or summarized from
the book. Technique cards do not award decision stars for a claimed physical
skill the software cannot observe.

| Card | Connected groups | Demonstration / optional adult-led activity |
|---|---|---|
| Touch and look | G01 | Animate a close first touch away from a cone; gently roll a ball to either foot and pause to look up |
| Two-foot explorer | G05 | Alternate highlighted feet in the demo; try a few gentle touches with each foot at a comfortable pace |
| Friendly delivery | G02, G03 | Compare short ground delivery with a longer intended path; roll or pass gently toward a partner who signals readiness |
| Keep it close | G05 | Show John between an opponent and the ball; use a stationary ball to discuss body position with an adult |
| Find the window | G04, G06 | Pause a shooting picture and point to available space; any physical attempt follows the coach's suitable activity |
| Watch a player | G08, G20 | Parent chooses a suitable clip, pauses before a pass and asks what the player can see; profile details remain pending |
| One good question | G21, G23 | Ask what changed the child's decision; choose one small practice goal together |

Curved/lofted delivery, volleys and aerial heading stay outside first-release
performance tasks. Track them in the coverage plan rather than making the
screen pretend to assess contact technique. The heading entry remains disabled
until the parent/coach and configured competition context permit its treatment.
