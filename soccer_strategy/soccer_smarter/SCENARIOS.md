## M01 - Find the exit before turning

Source status: OVERVIEW_ONLY / BOOK_BODY_PENDING; content: ORIGINAL.

John starts at (26,54), A at (26,47), BACK at (43,65). In A, A2 starts at
(45,50); both the side exit and the pass to BACK are good choices. The turn
follows (26,54) -> (26,58) -> (35,58), while A2 moves to (35,54), directly ahead.

real diagonal outlet. The forward carry is screened; pausing does not remove
that obstruction. Every bad carry stops on its intended forward line.

## M03 - Direct the restart

Source status: OVERVIEW_ONLY / BOOK_BODY_PENDING; content: ORIGINAL.

Coach-led ground-route practice, within the player's stated practice range;
all shown receivers are eligible. This does not teach a competition rule
profile. John starts at (48,20), RW at (64,23), BACK at (40,36), and the keeper
at (57,5). The target (44,0) is visibly marked before a choice.

In A, A at (46.5,12.5) blocks the low shot to that exact target. The pass to RW
is open; rebuilding backward is also safe. After the pass to RW, A moves to
(56,21.5), exactly on the original return line to John. John can move deeper
to (52,30), opening a new angle. Staying at (48,20) remains screened. Moving
close at (60,27) permits a short layoff but offers less separation; it is
workable rather than automatically wrong.

In B, A at (58,21.875) blocks John's original wide pass, while the marked

carry to (40,28) fit the second picture. The old blocked-lane verdict must not
be reused after the passer changes. Holding is a safe but less progressive
choice. A shot or interception can end a branch without another decision.

John can offer wide support at (66,47), drop to a deeper outlet, or move into

## M05 - Speed dribble and adapt

Source status: OVERVIEW_ONLY / BOOK_BODY_PENDING; content: ORIGINAL.

Book theme: speed dribbling in open space and adapting to recovery pressure.
John starts as right winger at (74,56), M at (54,44), F at (38,28), BACK at
(64,68), and A at (60,56) covering the flat inside cut.

In A, A2 starts inside at (60,26), leaving the right-wing runway to (74,34)
open. Pushing a longer speed-dribble touch down the wing gains ground quickly,
while A2 slides over to (74,24) to close the wing lane. On the second decision,
forcing another sprint straight ahead meets A2, whereas passing across to open F
at (38,28) uses the space A2 vacated.

In B, A2 already stands at (74,42) in the wing runway. Attempting a speed
dribble into a closed runway loses the ball at A2. Passing early inside to M at
(54,44) beats A2 while John overlaps to (70,32) and A2 shifts to (54,32) ahead
of M. Control switches to M, who can pass wide to overlapping John rather than
carry into A2.

## X01 - My playing principle

Source status: OVERVIEW_ONLY / BOOK_BODY_PENDING; content: ORIGINAL.

Book theme: personal soccer philosophy. Optional and unscored. Extend
one-good-question into a choose -> evidence -> revise loop. Offer principles
such as "Look before my next touch" and "Help the teammate with the ball";
allow skip or an editable short phrase. Do not rank principles.

A example: John chooses "Use the open route", links M02's first decision,
then completes "I noticed the straight lane was blocked." Show B's changed
blocker and invite a revision: "Check both routes each time." Feedback:
"Your idea now explains both situations." No star is added.

B contrast: no remembered mission or no wish to write. Offer a replay or
skip; never invent evidence or display a failed-goal badge. The observable
addition is preserving and revising an idea, rather than another adult prompt.

## X02 - My practice team

Source status: OVERVIEW_ONLY / BOOK_BODY_PENDING; content: ORIGINAL.

Book theme: support team and preparation. Optional and unscored. Build a
session-local plan with one goal, a helper role, an observation and a next step.
Example goal: "Notice the open side before turning." Helper options: coach,
teammate, trusted adult, or "ask later". No personal names are requested.

A example: choose the goal, choose "coach", and select "Ask them to point out
the exit after I explain mine." After a practice/replay, select an observation
such as "I looked up before choosing" or "I want another try"; then keep or
revise the plan. Feedback: "That observation gives you a useful next step."

B contrast: the helper is unavailable or the child does not want an activity.
Offer ask-later, change-plan and skip with identical tactical totals. Treat
rest, food and sleep as discussion themes for trusted adults; prescribe no
quantities and score no lifestyle choices. Offline physical technique practice
requires adult/coach guidance; these screens do not certify skill mastery.
