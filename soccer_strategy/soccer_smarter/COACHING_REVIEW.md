# Case review - fourteen episodes, twenty-eight decisions, eighty-four choices

Reviewed every choice against its cue, controlled player, intended route,
animation, successor and feedback on 2026-11-07. The previous green software
suite established execution, not tactical correctness. This is an authored
review, not certification by John's coach.

## Principles and limits

- Safe retention and forward progression depend on available space and opponents:
  [FIFA: Combine to progress play](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/talent-coach-programme/build-and-progress/possession-game-combine-to-progress-play.php).
- Scan, avoid pressure and offer usable passing angles:
  [FIFA: John Peacock, passing and receiving](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/elite-sessions/in-possession/john-peacock-passing-and-receiving.php).
- Moving after passing can change the receiving angle; reassess the new picture:
  [FIFA: Directing the ball with the first touch](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/elite-sessions/in-possession/directing-the-ball-with-the-first-touch.php).

These sources support principles, not our exact coordinates and ratings or the
unavailable book body. Coordinates are diagram units, not metres. Ground routes
are teaching schematics, not physical flight or live interception simulations.
The marked goal target is within the stated practice range; it does not prescribe
a real child's shooting distance or competition rules. Pictured receivers are
eligible in this drill. Multiple good choices are allowed.

## Corrections

1. M01.A accepts both an open pass and the turn. A2 actually closes the second
   forward lane. M01.B identifies BACK as the new controlled player, with John
   moving into a real outlet after releasing the pass.
2. M02 uses a gentler curve. Hover shows the intended receiver, while the
   intercepted ball stops on that path. CM's next decision is after receipt.
3. M03 starts closer to goal with a visible target and explicit ground routes.
   Staying in a screened return lane is no longer rated workable. A close layoff
   is workable instead of automatically wrong; a deeper angle gives more space.
4. M03.B's new passer opens the previously blocked wide route. That pass is
   accepted alongside the clear central carry.
5. M04.A's defender follows wide and blocks the right carry. M04.B screens the
   forward pass instead, leaving both the right carry and return pass available.
6. Off-ball previews show the controlled player's movement even when a teammate
   also passes. A good selected option remains the coach's demonstration.
7. Neutral labels describe intentions; static waiting no longer claims to
   demonstrate shielding. Feedback refers to actual visible positions.

## Verification boundary

Case tests check positive routes at 101 frames, real receiver endpoints,
contact-before-support movement, controlled dribbling, screened support,
intended-path prefixes, changed passing angles and multiple good options.
The clearance threshold checks diagram consistency, not real defensive reach
or the size of illustrated figures. SDL tests check intended hover routes,
truncated interceptions, visible targets and all scene/choice layouts.

Prediction and growth remain unscored. All missions remain ORIGINAL /
OVERVIEW_ONLY / BOOK_BODY_PENDING. Coach review, book-body alignment and a child
playtest remain separate from this completed code and diagram audit.
