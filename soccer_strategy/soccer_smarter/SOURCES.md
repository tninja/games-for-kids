## What content was actually checked

The publisher describes a sequel for children who know the basics, with over
50 techniques. It specifically names L-turns, curved kicks and free kicks.
Other stated themes are developing a playing philosophy, observing skills in
professional games, building a support team and preparing away from the pitch.
Its named player examples include Girma, Pulisic, Bonmati and Mbappe. The June
2026 *School Library Journal* review (Bob Hassett) additionally confirms three
broad book sections - off-field preparation/conditioning, individual techniques
with "Try It" solo drills and safe heading notes, and team strategies - and
specifically highlights speed dribbling, shooting inside the penalty box,
creating space, getting into scoring positions, and six-week goal reviews.
These are overview and review claims, not a complete verified chapter list.

## Provenance and later verification

Use two independent fields: `source_status=OVERVIEW_ONLY` and
`review_status=BOOK_BODY_PENDING`, alongside `content_provenance=ORIGINAL`.
Public descriptions verify the theme; they do not verify an authored answer,
animation, technique demonstration or whole-book completeness.

If lawful chapter access becomes available, record edition, exact heading and
locator, then check each associated mission with a coach. Mark only that
mapping BODY_CHECKED after review; keep original scenarios labeled ORIGINAL.
Do not propagate the 2016 book's page numbers or its G01-G23 curriculum into
the 2026 book. New chapters should pass the overlap audit before adding content.
The current design remains useful as an original advanced prototype if book
access never becomes available.
