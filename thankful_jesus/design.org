* Overview and Product Goals

*Thankful Journey with Jesus* (working title) is a standalone, pure client-side 2D warm-storybook Thanksgiving web game created for Christian church children ages 6-9, hosted on GitHub Pages under =docs/thankful_jesus/=.

- *Core Ministry and Game Pillars*:
  1. *Christ-Centered Thanksgiving*: Centers directly on Jesus through two beloved Gospel moments -- Jesus giving thanks and multiplying the boy's loaves and fish (John 6:1-14) and the healed traveler running back to Jesus' feet to say thank you (Luke 17:11-19).
  2. *Playful Bible Verse Memory*: Turns short Thanksgiving memory verses (John 6:11, Luke 17:15-16, 1 Thessalonians 5:18, Psalm 107:1) into collectible illustrated phrase tiles that kids assemble as a game reward rather than a classroom worksheet.
  3. *Live Bilingual Support and Web Speech Read-Aloud*: Supports instant =EN / ZH / Bilingual= switching (NIV/NIrV English + CUV Chinese) with browser Web Speech API read-aloud so 6-year-old early readers, bilingual church families, and Sunday school teachers can play and read Scripture together.
  4. *iPhone-First Tactile Fun*: Portrait-first single-hand touch ergonomics (=56px+= hit targets, safe-area aware, iOS Share Sheet PNG keepsake export), plus 2-4 player Pass-and-Play on one phone and clean projection support for church classrooms and laptops.
- *Visual Direction*: Warm, gentle, respectful 2D hand-drawn storybook illustration of Jesus and Gospel landscapes rendered via HTML5 Canvas, completely separate in codebase and art style from the secular family game in =thanksgiving/=.
- *Target Session Length*: 4 to 6 minutes per complete journey.
- *Constraints*: Zero backend, zero external image/audio fetches, strict TypeScript compiled via esbuild into an ASCII-clean UMD bundle matching =battleship=, =flappybird=, =foosball=, and =thanksgiving=.

* Bilingual Localization and ASCII-Safe Encoding Strategy

** Code-File Compliance and Runtime Rendering (=i18n.ts=)

To satisfy both the repository rule ("only use English in code files" / strictly ASCII bundled JS) and full English + Chinese runtime support:

- All TypeScript source files (=thankful_jesus/ts/*.ts=), identifiers, and comments remain strictly English and pure 7-bit ASCII.
- Chinese UI strings and CUV Bible verses live exclusively in =thankful_jesus/ts/i18n.ts= and =verses.ts= as standard JavaScript Unicode escape sequences (=\uXXXX=), accompanied by clear English comments stating the verse reference or UI meaning.
- Because =esbuild= runs with =charset: "ascii"=, both source files and the emitted =docs/thankful_jesus/app.js= pass the strict ASCII byte check while decoding natively into UTF-16 Chinese characters at runtime across DOM text, Canvas 2D typography, PNG keepsake cards, and =window.speechSynthesis=.
- Runtime custom prayer notes entered by kids naturally accept any Unicode input (English, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, punctuation).

** Language Modes and iPhone Narrow-Screen Rules

- =LangMode = "en" | "zh" | "dual"=, switchable live from the compact top bar (=EN | ZH | Dual=) at any screen without losing game progress.
- *Compact Action Buttons* (375px iPhone width): Primary action buttons keep concise 1-3 word labels in =en= or =zh=; in =dual= mode, buttons render crisp stacked 2-line labels (English primary + compact Chinese secondary) while staying within a comfortable =56-68px= thumb button height.
- *Scripture and Story Surfaces*: Stage story intros, Bible verse cards, Verse Builder tiles, church sharing prompts, and the exported PNG keepsake card render full English + Chinese side-by-side/stacked in =dual= mode so bilingual church teachers and parents can read Scripture together with kids.

* Level and Mini-Game Design (Ages 6-9 Scaling)

Every stage teaches itself visually in under 2 seconds, lasts 25-45 seconds, centers on Jesus, and rewards kids with Bible verse tokens.

** Stage 1 - Loaves & Fish Feast (=loaves_and_fish=, John 6:1-14)

- *Story Beat*: A young boy offers his five barley loaves and two fish to Jesus on a sunny hillside by the Sea of Galilee.
- *Step A (2-3s Prayer Ritual)*: Before gameplay begins, a glowing ="Give Thanks with Jesus!"= button invites the child to tap once; Jesus lifts the bread in prayer as warm golden rays shimmer over the basket.
- *Step B (25-35s Tactile Gameplay)*: Slide a thumb along the wide bottom track (or tap/hold =LEFT= / =RIGHT=; =A/D= or arrows on desktop) to guide the woven sharing basket across the hillside, catching multiplying loaves and fish to share with joyful seated families and collecting glowing *12-Basket Blessing Tokens* (carrying verse tiles).
- *Gentle Feedback*: Missed loaves land harmlessly on grass with a soft bounce; in *Little Explorer* mode, a friendly hillside breeze nudges golden blessing tokens toward the basket.
- *Goal*: Share 10 meals (Explorer) or 16 meals with streak bonuses (Adventurer, 35s timer). Unlocks *John 6:11*.

** Stage 2 - Run Back to Thank Jesus (=run_back_to_jesus=, Luke 17:11-19)

- *Story Beat*: Ten travelers are healed by Jesus on the road; nine rush straight on to busy village errands, while one pauses, turns around, and runs back to thank Jesus.
- *Phase A (20-25s Village Path)*: Tap Left/Center/Right lane buttons (or swipe left/right on the stage) to guide the healed traveler down a sunlit road, collecting glowing *Gratitude Sparkles* while sidestepping playful distractions (busy market carts, shiny toys, hurrying travelers that cause a gentle brief wobble, never instant fail).
- *Phase B (8-12s Turning Point Climax)*: At the village crossroads sign, the screen highlights a warm radiant button ="Turn Back to Jesus!"=. Rapidly tap the button (with animated footprints turning homeward) as the traveler runs joyfully back to Jesus' feet and praises God.
- *Goal*: Collect 8 sparkles + reach Jesus (Explorer) or 12 sparkles + rapid turn-back bonus (Adventurer, 32s timer). Unlocks *Luke 17:15-16*.

** Stage 3 - Thankful Verse Builder (=verse_builder=, 1 Thess 5:18 & Psalm 107:1)

- *Story Beat*: Open the golden treasure scroll filled with the Bible verse phrase tiles collected in Stages 1 and 2.
- *Gameplay*:
  - Kids assemble 2 short Thanksgiving memory verses (*Psalm 107:1* and *1 Thessalonians 5:18*; in Explorer mode, 1 primary verse + optional bonus verse).
  - Tapping any phrase tile plays a gentle chime AND optionally speaks that phrase aloud via Web Speech (huge support for 6-year-old early readers!).
  - Placing a tile in the right slot lights up the scroll with warm sparkles; tapping a wrong slot gently jiggles the tile and offers a friendly hint without losing progress.
  - *Bible Adventurer (Ages 8-9) Extra*: After each assembled verse, presents 1 warm, 3-choice story reflection question (e.g., "In Luke 17, what did the one thankful traveler do after Jesus healed him?"), reinforcing comprehension without grading anxiety.

** Finale - Thank-You Card to Jesus & Bilingual Keepsake PNG (=prayer_card= -> =journey_finale=)

- *Prayer Tree / Scroll*: Depicts warm storybook Jesus beside an olive/autumn Gratitude Tree overlooking Galilee.
- Each child (1-4 in Pass-and-Play, or "Add Another Prayer") chooses:
  1. *Ribbon / Leaf Color*: Sunlit Gold, Olive Green, Autumn Crimson, or Galilee Sky.
  2. *Illustrated Gratitude Sticker* (8 vector icons drawn on Canvas):
     - =jesus_love=: "Thank You Jesus for Your love!"
     - =bible=: "Thank You for the Bible!"
     - =family=: "Thank You for my family!"
     - =church=: "Thank You for my church friends!"
     - =food=: "Thank You for our daily food!"
     - =healing=: "Thank You for helping and healing us!"
     - =creation=: "Thank You for Your wonderful world!"
     - =joy=: "Thank You for joyful hearts!"
  3. *Optional Custom Prayer Note*: Single-line input (=maxlength="56"=) supporting English or Chinese.
- *Celebration & Offline Church Bridge*:
  - Displays the completed scene with a prominent ="Listen to Our Verse & Prayer"= Web Speech button.
  - Shows a shuffleable *Church & Table Sharing Prompt* (EN/ZH/Dual), e.g., "Turn to a friend or parent and share one way Jesus helped or blessed you this week!"
- *Client-Side 1080x1440 Bilingual PNG Keepsake Card (=postcard.ts=)*:
  - Offscreen 3:4 portrait Canvas card featuring the title banner, warm storybook Jesus & Gratitude Tree illustration, featured bilingual Bible verse (*1 Thessalonians 5:18* in selected language mode), and every child's prayer note cleanly word-wrapped.
  - Export path: =canvas.toBlob("image/png")= -> iOS 15+ =navigator.share({ files: [file] })= native Share Sheet -> fallback blob download + long-press =<img>= preview modal.

** Cross-Age Difficulty Matrix (Ages 6-9)

| Dimension | Little Explorer (Ages 6-7) | Bible Adventurer (Ages 8-9) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fail State | None (no countdown pressure; always >= 1 star) | Gentle timer + streak multipliers; completion always >= 1 star |
| Stage 1 & 2 Pacing | Wider catch/lane margins, slower speed, gentle token nudge | Crisp movement, bonus golden tokens, livelier distractions |
| Verse Builder Tiles | 4-5 chunky phrase tiles, color-matched icons, next-slot glow | 6-7 phrase tiles, no next-slot highlight, subtle decoy shuffle |

| Read-Aloud Support | Tap any tile or card to hear Web Speech EN/ZH/Bilingual | Same on-demand Listen buttons + optional auto-read toggle |

* Web Speech API Bilingual Read-Aloud and Synthesized Audio

** Web Speech Controller (=speech.ts=)

- *Zero External Assets*: Uses browser-native =window.speechSynthesis= and =SpeechSynthesisUtterance= so bundle size remains tiny and offline-friendly once loaded.
- *iOS Safari Reliability Engineering*:
  1. *Gesture Unlock*: Trigger speech directly inside =pointerdown=/=click= handlers on ="Listen"= buttons, verse tiles, or stage-start taps so iOS Safari never silently blocks audio.
  2. *Anti-Queue Cancellation*: Always call =speechSynthesis.cancel()= and increment a monotonic =speechGeneration= counter before speaking a new item, preventing runaway queues when kids tap rapidly.
  3. *Bilingual Chaining (=dual= mode)*: Speak the English utterance first (=en-US=, =rate: 0.92=, =pitch: 1.04=); inside =onend=, check that =speechGeneration= is still current and =!muted=, then speak the Chinese utterance (=zh-CN=, =rate: 0.90=, =pitch: 1.03=).
  4. *Voice Selection*: Cache voices via =getVoices()= and =voiceschanged=; prefer local non-robotic =en-US= and =zh-CN= (fallback =zh-TW= / =zh-HK= / any =zh*=) voices; fall back gracefully to default browser voice if specific locale voice is unavailable.
  5. *Graceful Degradation*: Feature-detect ="speechSynthesis" in window=; if unavailable (older WebViews or headless Node tests), hide or disable read-aloud indicators cleanly with zero console errors.
- *Classroom Etiquette*:
  - Default =autoRead: false= (explicit tap on large ="Listen"= speaker buttons) or opt-in toggle in the top bar, preventing unexpected sound bursts in a quiet church sanctuary.
  - Top-bar =Mute= button immediately cancels active speech (=speechSynthesis.cancel()=) and silences both Web Audio SFX and Web Speech.

** Synthesized Web Audio SFX (=audio.ts=)

- Lightweight procedural Web Audio chimes (matching =thanksgiving/ts/audio.ts=):
  - Soft warm harp arpeggio on "Give Thanks with Jesus" prayer tap
  - Gentle marimba pop when sharing loaves/fish or catching sparkles
  - Joyful ascending bell chime on turning back to Jesus and completing a Bible verse
  - Cheerful 5-note celebration chord on Journey Finale
- Lazily unlock =AudioContext= on first =pointerdown=; respect the same top-bar =muted= flag.
