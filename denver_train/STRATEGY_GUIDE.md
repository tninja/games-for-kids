# Train Adventure - Enhanced Strategy Guide

## Weather System 🌤️

### Weather Types and Effects:

**☀️ Sunny Weather**
- Speed: Normal (100%)
- Fuel: Normal consumption
- Passenger Mood: +10 satisfaction
- Best for: Any speed travel

**🌧️ Rainy Weather**
- Speed: 20% slower
- Fuel: 20% more consumption
- Passenger Mood: -5 satisfaction
- Risk: 20% chance of delays (15-45 min)

**❄️ Snowy Weather**
- Speed: 40% slower
- Fuel: 50% more consumption  
- Passenger Mood: -10 satisfaction
- Risk: 30% chance fast travel blocked!

**🌫️ Foggy Weather**
- Speed: 30% slower
- Fuel: 10% more consumption
- Passenger Mood: -3 satisfaction
- Risk: 15% chance of delays (10-25 min)

## Speed Strategy 🚂

### Speed Options:

**🐌 Slow Speed (30 mph)**
- Fuel: 20% less consumption
- Time: Takes longer
- Risk: Minimal weather risks
- Passengers: Some prefer slower travel

**🚶 Medium Speed (50 mph)**
- Fuel: Normal consumption
- Time: Balanced
- Risk: Moderate weather effects
- Passengers: Generally acceptable

**🏃 Fast Speed (70 mph)**
- Fuel: 40% more consumption
- Time: Faster arrival
- Risk: ⚠️ BLOCKED in snow storms!
- Passengers: Some enjoy speed, others don't

## Strategic Considerations

### Weather-Speed Combinations:
- ✅ **SAFE**: Any speed in sunny weather
- ⚠️ **CAUTION**: Fast speed in rain/fog (delays possible)
- ❌ **DANGER**: Fast speed in snow (30% chance of being blocked)

### Passenger Satisfaction:
- Weather affects all passenger moods
- Long delays (>30 min) make passengers very unhappy (-15)
- Some passengers have speed preferences
- Happy passengers give better tips!

### Fuel Management:
- Consider weather multipliers when planning fuel stops
- Snow travel can use 50% more fuel than expected
- Fast travel in bad weather = very expensive fuel costs

### Optimal Strategy:
1. **Check weather before choosing speed**
2. **Use slow/medium speed in bad weather**
3. **Plan extra fuel for snow travel**
4. **Monitor passenger satisfaction**
5. **Don't risk fast travel in snow storms**

## Tips for Success 🌟

1. **Weather Watching**: Always check current weather in the status panel
2. **Fuel Buffer**: Keep extra fuel for unexpected weather effects  
3. **Speed Selection**: Match speed to weather conditions
4. **Passenger Care**: Happy passengers = more money for fuel
5. **Risk Management**: Sometimes slower is better than being stuck!

The game is now much more strategic - weather and speed choices really matter for your success!
